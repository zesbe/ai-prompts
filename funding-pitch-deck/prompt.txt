hallowa.id = SaaS unified messaging B2B Indonesia (WA, IG, Messenger, Threads).

═══ RISET 2026 (FRAMEWORK) ═══

Sequoia framework 12-15 slide:
1. Company Purpose (1 sentence)
2. Problem
3. Solution
4. Why Now (timing)
5. Market Size (TAM/SAM/SOM)
6. Competition / Alternatives
7. Product (demo / screenshot)
8. Business Model
9. Traction / Metrics
10. Team
11. Financials / Projections
12. The Ask

YC simpler approach:
- Lebih singkat (10-12 slide)
- Heavy on traction numbers
- Less polish, more substance
- Direct & confident

B2B SaaS metric standard 2026:
- ARR & growth rate (target: 100%+ YoY untuk Series A)
- NRR (Net Revenue Retention): 110%+ excellent, 95%+ baseline
- Gross margin: 70%+ healthy
- LTV:CAC: 3:1 minimum
- Payback period: <12 bulan
- Magic Number: >0.75

Indonesia VC landscape:
- Series A typical: $5-15M
- Active local VC: East Ventures, AC Ventures, BRI Ventures,
  Mandiri Capital, MDI Ventures, Alpha JWC
- Regional: Sequoia SEA, Insignia, Wavemaker, Openspace
- Global: Lightspeed, Tiger, Accel (selective)

═══ DESIGN GUIDELINES ═══

- Visual style: clean, minimal, brand-consistent
- Font: 1-2 max, sans-serif (Inter, Helvetica)
- Color: HalloWa brand palette
- Per slide: 1 message, 1 visual, max 6 bullets
- Density: lebih banyak white space = lebih premium
- Charts: simple, label clearly, source cited
- Screenshots: high-res, no clutter
- Avoid: stock photos generic, walls of text, jargon excessive

═══ NARRATIVE ARC ═══

Story flow yang work untuk B2B SaaS:
1. Hook (problem yang relatable)
2. Tension (current solutions broken)
3. Resolution (your solution)
4. Validation (traction proves it)
5. Vision (where this goes — bigger market)
6. Ask (specific & confident)

Avoid:
- Starting with company history
- "We're building the [X] for [Y]" cliché
- Hype tanpa data
- Defensive tone soal kompetitor
