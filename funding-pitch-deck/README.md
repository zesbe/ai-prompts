# Funding Pitch Deck — hallowa.id Series A

Prompt untuk susun investor pitch deck (Series A target $5-15M) mengikuti Sequoia/YC framework dengan adaptasi konteks Indonesia.

## 🎯 Stage

- **Series A** (post-product market fit, scaling)
- **Target raise**: $5-15M (~Rp 75-225M)
- **Status hallowa**: 10K+ customers, 50M+ messages/month, Meta Partner

## 📋 14-Slide Structure (Sequoia-style)

1. Cover
2. Company Purpose
3. Problem
4. Solution
5. Why Now
6. Market Size (TAM/SAM/SOM)
7. Competition
8. Product
9. Business Model
10. Traction / Metrics
11. Go-to-Market
12. Team
13. Financials / Projections
14. The Ask

## 📊 B2B SaaS Metrics 2026 (yang investor cek)

| Metric | Series A Target |
|---|---|
| ARR growth | 100%+ YoY |
| NRR (Net Revenue Retention) | 110%+ excellent |
| Gross margin | 70%+ |
| LTV:CAC | 3:1 minimum |
| Payback period | <12 bulan |
| Magic Number | >0.75 |

## 🇮🇩 Indonesia VC Landscape

- **Local early-stage**: East Ventures, AC Ventures, BRI Ventures, Mandiri Capital, MDI Ventures, Alpha JWC
- **Regional**: Sequoia SEA, Insignia, Wavemaker, Openspace
- **Global selective**: Lightspeed, Tiger, Accel

## ⏱️ Timeline

- Week 1-2: Narrative + data collection
- Week 3-4: First draft + advisor review
- Week 5: Polish + pitch practice
- Week 6-8: Investor outreach
- Week 9-16: Meetings, term sheet
- Week 17-20: Diligence, closing
- **Total: 4-5 bulan typical Series A**
