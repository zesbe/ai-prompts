# Content Calendar 12 Weeks — hallowa.id

Prompt untuk susun content calendar 12 minggu yang align dengan SEO + GEO strategy, multi-channel (blog, LinkedIn, Instagram, email, webinar).

## 🎯 Why This Matters

Output Phase 5 dari [`seo-hallowa`](../seo-hallowa/). Tanpa editorial calendar, content production chaotic. Best practice 2026: **4 horizons planning** — Annual themes → Quarterly pillars → Monthly briefs → Weekly production.

## 📊 Distribusi Mix

- **40% TOFU** (awareness, broad)
- **35% MOFU** (educational, deep-dive)
- **25% BOFU** (comparison, case study, ROI calculator)

## 🏛️ 3 Pillars (12 minggu)

| Bulan | Pillar | Target |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | WhatsApp Business API | Foundational, high volume |
| 2 | Unified Messaging / Omnichannel | Position differentiator |
| 3 | CS Automation | Sinergi dengan Visual Flow Builder launch |

## 📅 Per-Week Output

- 1-2 blog post (pillar atau cluster)
- 3 LinkedIn post
- 3 Instagram post + reel
- 1 email newsletter
- 1 webinar (per bulan)

## 💰 Team
