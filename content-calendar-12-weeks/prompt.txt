═══ CONTENT TEMPLATE PER POST TYPE ═══

A. PILLAR BLOG POST (3000-4000 kata)
   Structure:
   - TL;DR di awal (3-5 sentences) — KRUSIAL untuk GEO
   - Table of contents
   - Definition + context
   - Step-by-step / how-to (with images)
   - Use cases (3-5 examples)
   - Common mistakes / FAQ
   - Comparison vs alternatives
   - ROI / results data
   - CTA: trial signup atau demo
   
   GEO optimization:
   - FAQ schema markup
   - Citable statistics (with sources)
   - Author bio dengan kredensial
   - Internal links 5-8 ke cluster

B. CLUSTER BLOG POST (1200-2000 kata)
   - Direct answer di awal (TL;DR)
   - Step-by-step atau analytical
   - Internal link ke pillar
   - CTA spesifik untuk topik

C. COMPARISON / ALTERNATIVES PAGE (2500 kata)
   - Comparison table (visual hierarchy)
   - Per-criteria breakdown
   - Pros/cons honest (jangan terlalu sales-y)
   - Conclusion: hallowa best for X, kompetitor best for Y
   - CTA trial gratis
   - FAQ schema

D. LINKEDIN POST (max 1300 char untuk algoritma optimal)
   Hook + insight + CTA singkat
   Format yang work:
   - Numbered list (10x lipat reach vs paragraph)
   - Before/after story
   - Contrarian take
   - Personal experience founder

E. INSTAGRAM CAROUSEL (5-10 slide)
   Slide 1: Hook (problem/question)
   Slide 2-8: Insight per slide (1 idea per slide)
   Slide 9: CTA (link in bio)
   Slide 10: Logo/branding

F. INSTAGRAM REEL (15-60s)
   3-second hook + body + CTA
   Captions WAJIB (banyak silent viewer)

G. EMAIL NEWSLETTER (weekly digest)
   - 1 main story
   - 3 link curated (blog post, news, tool)
   - 1 CTA (try feature, register webinar, dll)

H. WEBINAR (1 per bulan)
   45 menit content + 15 menit Q&A
   Replay di-distribute via blog, email, LinkedIn
