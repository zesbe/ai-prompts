# Partner Program Design — hallowa.id

Prompt untuk design partner ecosystem (affiliate + reseller + integration partner). Goal: 30% revenue dari partner dalam 12 bulan.

## 🎯 Why This Matters

Scaling pure direct sales = expensive. Partner channel = sustainable distribution tanpa hire 2x sales team.

## 📊 Riset 2026

- 56% partner program <50 affiliate
- 7.6% affiliate generate 1+ referral
- Recurring commission > one-time
- **Triple-Threat 2026**: Resellers + Affiliates + Referrals

## 💰 Commission Structure (Recommended)

| Type | Commission | Commitment |
|---|---|---|
| Affiliate | 20% recurring × 12 bulan | Low (just promote) |
| Reseller | 25% recurring + markup | Min 5 deal/quarter |
| Integration | Negotiated rev share 10-20% | Joint engineering |
| Referral | 1 bulan gratis | Zero (existing customer) |

## 🛠️ Tooling

- **Bootstrap**: Rewardful ($49-129/mo) atau Tapfiliate
- **Mid**: PartnerStack ($500+/mo)
- **Enterprise**: Allbound, Channeltivity

## 🎯 Targets 12 Bulan

- 100+ active affiliate
- 20+ reseller
- 5+ integration partner
- 30% revenue dari partner channel
- Partner CAC 50% lebih rendah dari direct
