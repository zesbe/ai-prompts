Tolong design partner program (reseller + affiliate + integration partner) untuk hallowa.id. Goal: bangun channel distribution sustainable yang generate 30%+ revenue dari partner dalam 12 bulan.

═══ KONTEKS ═══
hallowa.id = SaaS unified messaging B2B Indonesia.
Saat ini: pure direct sales / PLG. Untuk scale tanpa hire 2x sales team, butuh partner ecosystem.

═══ RISET 2026 (BENCHMARK) ═══

State of SaaS Affiliate 2026:
- 56% partner program <50 affiliate
- Hanya 10% scale beyond 1000 affiliate
- 7.6% affiliates generate 1+ referral (bottom 92% silent)
- Recurring commission paling efektif (vs one-time)
- Average lifetime: 18 bulan untuk SaaS Affiliate

Triple-Threat Ecosystem 2026:
- **Resellers** (technical expertise, sell + implement)
- **Affiliates** (high-velocity traffic, content/influencer)
- **Referrals** (high-trust loyalty, customer-to-customer)

Best practices:
- Tracking transparan via dashboard
- Onboarding flow yang clear
- Community building
- Competitive commission (15-30% recurring untuk SaaS)
- Active enablement (training, materials)

═══ DELIVERABLE YANG DIHARAPKAN ═══

1. PARTNER TYPE STRATEGY

   A. AFFILIATE PARTNERS (highest volume, lowest friction)
      Profil:
      - Content creator B2B (LinkedIn influencer, blogger, YouTuber)
      - Konsultan freelance / agency kecil
      - Komunitas (TDA, marketers, e-commerce groups)
      
      Commission: 20% recurring untuk 12 bulan, atau 30% one-time
      Commitment: low (just promote)
      Training: minimal (kasih asset + tracking link)

   B. RESELLER PARTNERS (mid-volume, high value)
      Profil:
      - Digital agency yang handle UMKM client
      - System Integrator (SI) yang implement business software
      - Consultant CRM / Marketing
      
      Commission: 25% recurring + 10% margin reseller markup
      Commitment: minimum 5 deal/quarter
      Training: certification program (4-8 jam)
      Benefits: dedicated CSM, white-label option (Enterprise),
        co-marketing fund

   C. INTEGRATION / TECH PARTNERS (strategic, ekosistem)
      Profil:
      - CRM/ERP players (Mekari sister product, JUBELIO,
        Forstok, Sirclo)
      - E-commerce platform (Shopify, WooCommerce, Tokopedia
        Mitra app)
      - Marketing tools (Mailchimp Indonesia, Mailerlite local)
      - Payment gateway (Xendit, Midtrans, GoPay merchant)
      
      Commission: tergantung deal — biasanya rev share 10-20%
        atau co-sell motion
      Commitment: integration build + co-marketing
      Training: technical workshops

   D. REFERRAL PROGRAM (existing customer drive)
      Profil: customer happy yang refer ke peer
      Reward: 1 bulan gratis untuk referrer + 50% off bulan 1
        untuk referee
      Commitment: zero
      Distribution: in-app banner, email campaign, NPS survey
        follow-up

2. COMMISSION STRUCTURE DETAIL

   Affiliate:
   - Rp 199k Starter: 20% × 12 bulan = Rp 478.4k total per signup
   - Rp 599k Pro: 20% × 12 bulan = Rp 1.4jt total per signup
   - Enterprise: custom (bisa $1000-5000 per deal)
   - Cookie window: 90 hari
   - Payout: monthly via bank transfer / ewallet
   - Minimum payout: Rp 500k
   - Anti-fraud: dedup IP, manual review for spike

   Reseller:
   - Rp 199k Starter: 25% × 12 bulan = Rp 597k + reseller markup
   - Rp 599k Pro: 25% × 12 bulan = Rp 1.8jt + reseller markup
   - Enterprise: tier-based (5-30% based on deal size)
   - Annual MDF (market development fund): Rp 5-50jt depending tier
   - Volume bonus: 10% extra setelah 20 deal/year

   Integration Partner:
   - Rev share negotiated case-by-case
   - Co-marketing budget shared 50/50
   - Joint pipeline tracked

   Referral:
   - 1 bulan gratis (max value Rp 599k)
   - Cap: 5 referral per customer per quarter (anti-abuse)

3. PARTNER PORTAL & TOOLING

   Tools options:
   - **Tapfiliate** ($89-299/mo) — affiliate tracking, payouts
   - **Rewardful** ($49-129/mo) — Stripe-native, simple
   - **PartnerStack** ($500+/mo) — full PRM for resellers
   - **Allbound, Channeltivity** — enterprise PRM
   - **Custom build** — kalau butuh integrasi mendalam dengan
     hallowa core
   
   Saranin: start dengan Rewardful atau Tapfiliate untuk affiliate,
   custom-light untuk reseller (Notion + Stripe + manual
   payouts) sampai 50+ partner.

   Portal harus punya:
   - Dashboard: deal in progress, total earned, paid, pending
   - Marketing assets: logo, banner, copy templates, case study
   - Sales enablement: pitch deck, ROI calculator, comparison
   - Training materials: video, certification quiz
   - Support: ticketing system khusus partner
   - Community: Slack/Discord channel untuk partner

4. PARTNER ONBOARDING FLOW

   Affiliate (low-touch, self-serve):
   1. Apply via web form
   2. Auto-approve kalau memenuhi criteria (LinkedIn 1k+, blog
      domain authority 20+)
   3. Welcome email + dashboard access
   4. 5-min onboarding video
   5. First-week kit: 5 ready-to-share posts, tracking link
   6. Monthly newsletter dengan asset + tips

   Reseller (high-touch, vetted):
   1. Application form (company info, target customer, capacity)
   2. Discovery call (30 min) untuk fit assessment
   3. Approval → contract sign
   4. Onboarding workshop (4 jam) — product demo, sales pitch,
      objection handling
   5. Certification quiz (must pass 80%)
   6. First deal coaching (CSM joins call)
   7. Quarterly business review

   Integration Partner (strategic, custom):
   1. Outbound atau inbound interest
   2. Discovery → fit → MOU
   3. Technical scoping
   4. Joint roadmap
   5. Integration build (joint engineering)
   6. Co-launch GTM
   7. Quarterly co-marketing review

   For Affiliate:
   - Tracking link generator

   - 10 social media post templates (LinkedIn, Instagram, X)
   - 5 email templates

   - "How to talk about HalloWa" 1-page primer

   For Reseller:
   - Pitch deck (template, customizable per industry)
   - ROI calculator
   - Comparison vs Qontak/Qiscus/Wati
   - Demo video (15-min)
   - Objection handling cheat sheet
   - SOW template

6. RECRUITMENT STRATEGY

   Affiliate (target: 100 dalam 6 bulan):
   - Outreach ke LinkedIn influencer B2B (top 100 di
     Indonesia messaging/CRM/marketing space)
   - Partner directories (Refgrow, Tapfiliate marketplace)
   - Content marketing: "Affiliate Program Hallowa" page
   - Komunitas: TDA, Indonesia Marketers, e-commerce groups
   - Customer referral: existing customer who's also creator

   Reseller (target: 20 dalam 6 bulan):
   - Outreach ke top 50 digital agency Indonesia
   - SI partner program (Niagahoster, BizNet, Telkomsigma)
   - LinkedIn Sales Navigator targeting
   - Industry events (Marketeers, IDB Indonesia, ISMG)
   - Existing customer who has agency ambitions

   Integration (target: 5 strategic dalam 12 bulan):
   - Direct outreach ke C-level platform target
   - Strategic introduction via investor / advisor
   - Industry event networking
   - Build first integration as POC, use as case study

7. PARTNER TIER STRUCTURE

   Bronze → Silver → Gold → Platinum

   Bronze (default):
   - Standard commission
   - Self-serve resources
   - Email support

   Silver (after 5 deal/year atau Rp 50jt commission):
   - +5% commission
   - Priority email support
   - Quarterly strategy call

   Gold (after 20 deal/year atau Rp 200jt):
   - +10% commission total
   - Dedicated CSM
   - MDF Rp 10jt/year
   - Co-marketing opportunities
   - Featured in directory

   Platinum (after 50 deal/year atau Rp 500jt):
   - +15% commission total
   - Dedicated AE + CSM
   - MDF Rp 50jt/year
   - Strategic account access
   - Annual offsite invite
   - Co-branded marketing campaigns

8. CHANNEL CONFLICT MANAGEMENT

   Rules:
   - Direct sales registers deal di CRM dulu = direct gets credit
   - Affiliate / reseller registers deal in partner portal = partner
     gets credit
   - 90-day cookie/protection window
   - Conflict resolution: 50/50 split kalau bersamaan + escalate
   - No "stealing" leads existing customer (anti-cannibalization)

9. METRICS & GOVERNANCE

   Track per quarter:
   - Total partners (active vs dormant)
   - Revenue from partner channel (% of total)
   - Customer acquired via partner (CAC comparison vs direct)
   - Partner satisfaction (NPS quarterly survey)
   - Activation rate (% partner yang gen 1+ referral)
   - Retention rate (partner churn)
   - Commission paid out vs revenue generated
   - LTV of partner-acquired customer vs direct

   Governance:
   - Monthly internal partner ops review
   - Quarterly partner advisory board (top 10 partners)
   - Annual partner conference

10. ROLLOUT PLAN

    Month 1: Setup tooling (Rewardful), legal (terms, agreements),
             internal alignment
    Month 2: Launch affiliate program (soft launch with 10 close
             contacts as pilot)
    Month 3: Open public affiliate, recruit 50 partners
    Month 4: Launch reseller program with 5 vetted agency
    Month 5-6: Scale recruitment, optimize onboarding based on
              feedback
    Month 7-9: Build first integration partner, co-launch
    Month 10-12: Refine tier structure, launch referral program
                 (in-app)
