Tolong setup competitive intelligence monitoring system untuk hallowa.id agar tau setiap pergerakan kompetitor (Mekari Qontak, Qiscus, Wati, YCloud, SleekFlow) secara real-time tanpa effort manual.

═══ KONTEKS ═══
Pasar B2B SaaS messaging Indonesia bergerak cepat. Kompetitor bisa launch fitur baru, ubah pricing, atau push campaign besar tanpa hallowa tau. Tanpa monitoring, hallowa selalu reaktif.

Goal: dapat alert otomatis dalam 24 jam saat kompetitor:
- Launch fitur baru
- Ubah pricing
- Bikin campaign marketing besar
- Hire executive baru (signal strategy shift)
- Raise funding
- Akuisisi / di-akuisisi
- Muncul di AI search untuk query target hallowa

- **Visualping** ($10-50/month) — website change detection
- **Klue** (enterprise, $$$) — battlecards + win/loss + AI summary
- **Crayon** (enterprise) — full CI platform

- **Semrush** ($129-499/month) — SEO + competitor keyword
- **Ahrefs** ($129-449/month) — backlink + content

- **Brand24, Mention** — social listening

Insight 2026:

- Win/loss analysis = struktural, bukan ad-hoc
- Job posting = leading indicator strategy shift

═══ DELIVERABLE YANG DIHARAPKAN ═══

1. MONITORING DIMENSIONS

   A. WEBSITE CHANGES (daily check)
      - /pricing — alert kalau ada perubahan tier/harga
      - /features — alert fitur baru
      - /integrations — alert integrasi baru
      - Homepage hero/CTA — alert positioning shift
      - Blog new posts — track content velocity & topics
      - /careers — job posting baru = signal team growth area
      - /case-studies — customer success stories baru
   
   B. PRODUCT CHANGES (weekly check)
      - Changelog/release notes
      - In-app announcements (kalau ada akses trial)
      - Help center / docs untuk fitur baru
      - GitHub public repo activity (kalau ada SDK/API publik)
   
   C. MARKETING & PR (real-time)
      - Press release / news mentions
      - Social media (LinkedIn company page, Instagram, X)
      - Paid ads tracking (Facebook Ad Library, LinkedIn Ad Library,
        Google Ads via SpyFu)
      - Webinar / event announcement
      - Partnership / integration announcement
   
   D. SEO & RANKING (weekly)
      - Top 50 target keyword ranking
      - New blog post yang muncul di top 10
      - Backlink baru (high-authority domain)
      - AI search citation (ChatGPT, Perplexity, Gemini, Claude
        manual probe)
   
   E. BUSINESS SIGNALS (monthly)
      - Funding announcement (Crunchbase alert)
      - Executive hire/leave (LinkedIn, news)
      - Office expansion (LinkedIn job posting per location)
      - Layoff / restructuring news
      - Acquisition rumor / actual
   
   F. CUSTOMER VOICE (weekly)
      - G2 review baru (positive + negative)
      - Capterra review baru
      - TrustPilot review (less common B2B)
      - Reddit mention (r/SaaS, r/UMKMIndonesia, lokal komunitas)
      - LinkedIn customer testimonial / complaint
      - Twitter/X mention monitoring

   - Google Alerts (free)

   - LinkedIn manual follow + notification

   - Similarweb free (limited data)
   
   Tier B — Lean ($50-200/month, ~Rp 750k-3jt):
   - Visualping paid ($10-50/mo)

   - SpyFu ($39/mo)

   Tier C — Mid ($300-800/month, ~Rp 4.5-12jt):

3. AUTOMATION SETUP

   - Visualping: setup 10-15 page kompetitor untuk track perubahan
   - Slack/Discord webhook: alert dikirim otomatis ke channel
     #competitive-intel
   - Notion/Airtable: dashboard agregat semua signal
   - Zapier/Make: glue automation antara tools
   - Weekly digest email: ringkasan otomatis semua signal minggu itu
   - Monthly report: deep analysis trends & implication

4. RESPONSE PLAYBOOK

   Untuk setiap jenis signal, kasih playbook:
   
   - **Kompetitor launch fitur baru:**
     1. Lab assess: gimana pengaruhnya ke positioning hallowa?
     2. Customer impact: ada churn risk?
     3. Response: do-nothing / catch-up / counter-position?
     4. Timeline: respond dalam 1-2 minggu
   
   - **Kompetitor turun harga / promo:**
     1. Cek apakah promo terbatas atau permanen
     2. Customer ada yang nanya/ngotot? Cek CRM
     3. Response: match? value-add? wait-and-see?
     4. Timeline: respond dalam 48 jam (revenue impact)
   
   - **Kompetitor raise funding besar:**
     1. Implikasi: budget marketing & hiring akan naik
     2. Strategi: defensive moat? pre-emptive PR?
   
   - **Kompetitor hire C-level baru (CMO, CRO):**
     1. Background research: dari mana?
     2. Implikasi strategy shift dalam 6 bulan
   
   - **Kompetitor mention buruk di review/social:**
     1. Sales enablement: kirim ke tim sales sebagai battlecard
     2. Reach out ke customer yang affected (kalau ada signal di
        CRM)

5. BATTLECARD DELIVERY

   Per kompetitor utama (Qontak, Qiscus, Wati):
   - One-pager battlecard untuk sales team
   - Update setiap bulan atau kalau ada perubahan major
   - Sections:
     * Strengths kompetitor
     * Weaknesses (untuk attack)
     * Pricing comparison
     * Feature parity matrix
     * Customer typical complaints
     * Common objection handling
     * Sample win/loss reasons

6. WIN/LOSS ANALYSIS PROCESS

   - Track setiap deal yang lost dengan reason category:
     * Lost ke kompetitor (specific kompetitor)
     * Lost ke status quo (no decision)
     * Lost ke budget
     * Lost ke timing
     * Lost ke fit
   - Quarterly review: top 3 reason loss → roadmap input
   - Quarterly review: top 3 reason win → marketing message reinforce

7. AI SEARCH VISIBILITY MONITORING (NEW 2026)

   Manual probe weekly (ChatGPT, Perplexity, Gemini, Claude):
   Query yang harus di-test:
   - "platform whatsapp business api terbaik di indonesia"
   - "alternatif qontak"
   - "alternatif qiscus"
   - "platform unified messaging untuk umkm indonesia"
   - "harga whatsapp business api indonesia"
   - "berapa biaya platform messaging untuk bisnis kecil"
   
   Track per query:
   - Apakah hallowa muncul?
   - Posisi di citation order?
   - Snippet text apa yang dikutip?
   - Kompetitor mana yang muncul lebih dulu?
   
   Otomasi: pakai texta.ai atau custom script (karena AI search
   API berkembang)

8. INTERNAL DISTRIBUTION

   - **Daily digest** ke product team (perubahan website kompetitor)
   - **Weekly digest** ke marketing team (content, campaign, SEO)
   - **Weekly digest** ke sales team (battlecards, objection updates)
   - **Monthly executive summary** ke C-level (strategic implications)
   - **Quarterly board update** (competitive landscape shift)
