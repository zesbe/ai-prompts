# Feature Gap Analysis — hallowa.id

Prompt komprehensif untuk lakukan **feature gap analysis** hallowa.id vs kompetitor B2B SaaS unified messaging Indonesia, plus build roadmap 12 bulan.

## 🎯 Tujuan

Identify fitur yang missing, validate dengan customer needs, prioritize pakai RICE framework, dan output roadmap yang executable.

## 📊 Hasil Riset Awal (gap yang udah teridentifikasi)

Berdasarkan riset 2026 vs Mekari Qontak, Qiscus, SleekFlow, ChakraHQ, AiSensy:

### 🔥 Critical Gaps (deal-breaker untuk B2B)

| # | Fitur | Kompetitor punya |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Visual Chatbot/Flow Builder | Qontak, Qiscus, SleekFlow |
| 2 | Unified Customer Profile (merge contacts) | Qiscus, Qontak |
| 3 | CRM essentials (tags, notes, custom fields) | Qontak (full CRM) |
| 4 | Bot-to-human handover dengan context | Qontak, Qiscus |
| 5 | Auto-routing (by topic/team/skill) | Qontak, Qiscus |
| 6 | Knowledge Base for agents | Qontak, Qiscus |
| 7 | Quick replies library | Standar industri |
| 8 | Working hours / out-of-office | Standar |

### ⚠️ Important Gaps

- CSAT/NPS surveys, Mobile app (iOS/Android), Sentiment analysis, Voice notes transcription, Auto-translate, Chat history export, Tags & labels, Audit logs

### 💡 Nice-to-Have / Differentiator

- App Marketplace, Industry templates, WhatsApp Coexistence, No-code AI Agent Builder, White-label, Email/SMS/Live chat channels, WhatsApp Catalog

### 🇮🇩 Indonesia-Specific (KRITIS untuk local market)

| Fitur | Why Critical |
|---|---|
| **Tokopedia integration** | UMKM Indonesia ada di sini |
| **Shopee integration** | E-commerce dominan |
| **Lazada integration** | Coverage lengkap |

## 📋 Methodology (4-Phase)

| Phase | Fokus | Durasi |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Desk Research (crawl, review platforms) | 1-2 hari |
| 2 | Deep-Dive Kompetitor (free trial, document UX) | 2-3 hari |
| 3 | Customer Validation (5-10 interview, ticket review) | 3-5 hari |
| 4 | Synthesis & Roadmap (RICE prioritization) | 2 hari |

**Total:** 8-12 hari, budget tools + insentif Rp 3-5jt.

## 🚀 Cara Pakai

## 💡 Tip Strategis

Jangan langsung ship semua gap. **Validate demand dulu** sebelum invest:

1. **Interview customer** sebelum bikin (50% asumsi internal salah)
2. **MVP first** — ship versi minimal, lihat usage, baru iterate
3. **Pricing-aware** — fitur enterprise jangan ditaro di Free tier
4. **Indonesia-first** — kalau bisa pilih, prioritize fitur Indonesia-specific
   karena itu moat yang sulit di-copy global player
