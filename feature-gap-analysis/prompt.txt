Tolong lakukan feature gap analysis komprehensif untuk hallowa.id vs kompetitor B2B SaaS unified messaging di pasar Indonesia. Tujuan: identifikasi fitur yang missing, prioritas pengembangan, dan roadmap 12 bulan ke depan.

═══ KONTEKS BISNIS ═══
hallowa.id adalah SaaS unified messaging platform B2B di Indonesia.
- Produk: integrasi WhatsApp Business API, Instagram, Messenger, Threads
- Status: Official Meta Business Partner
- Target: UMKM sampai enterprise di Indonesia
- Pricing: Free / Starter Rp199k / Pro Rp599k / Enterprise

═══ FITUR YANG SUDAH ADA DI HALLOWA.ID (BASELINE) ═══
- Unified Inbox (WA, IG, Messenger, Threads)
- Auto-Reply dengan AI (keyword matching + AI context)
- Multi-Agent (assignment + role management)
- Scheduled Posts (IG, FB, Threads)
- Analytics & Insights (response time, engagement)
- Enterprise Security (AES-256, 2FA, SOC 2, server Indonesia)
- WhatsApp: QR login, broadcast, interactive buttons, templates
- Instagram: DM inbox, story mentions, comment replies
- Messenger: FB Page inbox, automated responses, rich media
- Threads: Post scheduling, mention tracking
- API access (Pro tier+)

Pricing tiers:
- Free: 100 kontak, 1 nomor WA, 1 user, auto-reply dasar
- Starter (Rp199k): 1k kontak, 2 nomor WA, 3 user, auto-reply+AI,
  IG&Messenger, broadcast, analytics
- Pro (Rp599k): 10k kontak, 5 nomor WA, 10 user, semua + Threads,
  scheduled posts, full API access
- Enterprise: Unlimited, dedicated infra, custom integration, SLA 99.99%

═══ KOMPETITOR YANG HARUS DIBENCHMARK ═══

Tier 1 (Indonesia, paling agresif):
- Mekari Qontak (qontak.com) — pemain terbesar B2B SaaS messaging
- Qiscus (qiscus.com) — official BSP, banyak fitur enterprise
- Wati.io — fokus WhatsApp Business API

Tier 2 (regional/global):
- YCloud, 360Dialog, Bird CRM, SleekFlow, Trengo, Verloop.io, Zoko,
  AiSensy, ChakraHQ

Untuk masing-masing kompetitor, audit:
- Halaman /features atau /pricing untuk daftar fitur lengkap
- Changelog / blog "new feature" terbaru
- Industry-specific solutions yang ditawarkan
- Pricing tier breakdown (apa yang masuk Free vs Premium)
- Mobile app availability (iOS, Android)
- Marketplace / app integration

═══ FRAMEWORK GAP ANALYSIS ═══

Klasifikasi setiap fitur ke 4 kategori:

🔥 CRITICAL — Deal-breaker untuk B2B serious:
- Visual Chatbot/Flow Builder (drag-drop)
- Unified Customer Profile (merge contacts dari semua channel)
- CRM essentials (tags, notes, custom fields, segments)
- Bot-to-human handover dengan context
- Auto-routing (by topic/team/skill)
- Knowledge Base for agents
- Quick replies library
- Working hours / out-of-office automation

⚠️ IMPORTANT — Kompetitif disadvantage:
- CSAT/NPS post-conversation surveys
- Mobile app (iOS, Android) untuk agent
- Webhook/API documentation lengkap
- Sentiment analysis AI
- Voice notes transcription
- Auto-translate
- Chat history export (compliance)
- Tags & labels untuk conversations
- Audit logs lengkap

💡 NICE-TO-HAVE — Differentiator:
- App Marketplace / Extensibility
- Industry-specific templates (vertical solutions)
- WhatsApp Coexistence (personal + API)
- No-code AI Agent Builder
- White-label / reseller option
- Email channel integration
- Live chat widget (web)
- SMS channel
- Voice/calling capabilities
- WhatsApp Catalog + Products

🇮🇩 INDONESIA-SPECIFIC — Local market lock-in:
- Tokopedia chat integration
- Shopee chat integration
- Lazada chat integration
- TikTok Shop integration
- Local payment: GoPay, OVO, DANA, ShopeePay, QRIS
- Bahasa Indonesia NLP out-of-box
- PDP UU compliance explicit (UU 27/2022)
- Centang Hijau / Blue Tick verification service
- Indonesian holiday calendar untuk auto-out-of-office
- Multi-warehouse / multi-cabang support

   Q1 (Critical): close deal-breaker gaps
   Q2 (Indonesia lock-in): marketplace integration, payment, mobile app
   Q3 (Differentiator): app marketplace, AI agent builder, vertical
   Q4 (Polish & Scale): white-label, enterprise features

   Per quarter:
   - List fitur (3-5 max per quarter, jangan terlalu ambisius)
   - Estimasi development effort (man-months)
   - Estimasi revenue impact / churn reduction
   - Dependencies antar fitur
   - GTM plan (announcement, blog post, customer comms)

4. **Pricing Tier Re-design**
   Berdasarkan fitur baru, evaluate apakah tier sekarang masih masuk
   akal. Possible re-design:
   - Apakah Starter terlalu sempit (cuma 1k kontak)?
   - Apakah Pro terlalu mahal jump (Rp199k → Rp599k = 3x)?
   - Apakah perlu add tier "Growth" (Rp350-450k) di tengah?
   - Fitur mana yang harus jadi add-on (per-use pricing) vs include
     di tier?

5. **Competitive Positioning Statement**
   Setelah analisis, kasih saran positioning hallowa yang clear:
   - "vs Qontak: hallowa lebih [X], Qontak lebih [Y]"
   - "vs Qiscus: hallowa lebih [X], Qiscus lebih [Y]"
   - "vs Wati: hallowa lebih [X], Wati lebih [Y]"
   - Sweet spot hallowa (siapa best customer profile?)

6. **Risk Register**
   - Fitur apa yang KALAU GAK di-ship dalam 6 bulan, hallowa bakal
     kehilangan customer ke kompetitor?
   - Fitur apa yang risk overengineering (overinvest tanpa demand)?
   - Threat dari Big Tech (Meta langsung ke business, Google enter market)?
   - Pricing pressure dari new entrant murah?

═══ METODOLOGI RISET ═══

PHASE 1 — DESK RESEARCH (1-2 hari):
1. Crawl halaman /features, /pricing, /integrations dari semua
   kompetitor list di atas
2. Crawl changelog / blog untuk fitur terbaru 6 bulan
3. Cek G2, Capterra reviews untuk masing-masing kompetitor:
   - Top "loved" features (apa yang customer pakai paling sering)
   - Top "hated" issues (apa yang customer komplain)
4. Cek r/SaaS, r/UMKMIndonesia, Quora untuk pattern feature request

PHASE 2 — DEEP-DIVE KOMPETITOR (2-3 hari):
1. Sign up free trial di 3-5 kompetitor utama (atau pakai demo video
   YouTube kalau gak bisa)
2. Document UI/UX flow kunci:
   - Onboarding flow
   - Setup WhatsApp connection
   - Build chatbot/flow
   - Send broadcast
   - Multi-agent workflow
   - Reporting/analytics dashboard
3. Identify feature yang UX-nya superior atau inferior dibanding
   hallowa

PHASE 3 — CUSTOMER VALIDATION (3-5 hari):
1. Interview 5-10 existing hallowa customer:
   - Fitur paling sering dipakai
   - Fitur yang dipakai tapi UI-nya susah
   - Fitur yang DIBUTUHIN tapi BELUM ADA (top of list)
   - Pernah pertimbangkan switch ke kompetitor? Kenapa?
2. Cek support tickets / user feedback:
   - Top 20 feature request
   - Top 10 churn reason
3. Cek failed sales (kalau ada CRM):
   - Alasan utama lead pilih kompetitor

PHASE 4 — SYNTHESIS & ROADMAP (2 hari):
1. Sintesa semua data jadi feature matrix
2. Prioritize pakai RICE framework:
   - Reach: berapa customer affected
   - Impact: dampak per customer (1-10)
   - Confidence: validity of estimates (low/medium/high)
   - Effort: development effort (person-months)
   - Score = (R × I × C) / E
3. Roadmap draft → review dengan stakeholder
4. Final roadmap + GTM plan
