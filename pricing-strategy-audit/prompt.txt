Tolong susun pricing strategy audit komprehensif untuk hallowa.id berdasarkan benchmark B2B SaaS 2026 dan competitor analysis. Goal: tier yang convert lebih baik, ARPU lebih tinggi, dan churn lebih rendah.

═══ KONTEKS ═══
hallowa.id = SaaS unified messaging B2B Indonesia.
Pricing saat ini:
- Free: Rp 0 (100 kontak, 1 nomor WA, 1 user, auto-reply dasar)
- Starter: Rp 199k/bulan (1k kontak, 2 nomor WA, 3 user, broadcast,
  IG&Messenger)
- Pro: Rp 599k/bulan (10k kontak, 5 nomor WA, 10 user, Threads,
  scheduled posts, full API)
- Enterprise: Custom

Issue yang teridentifikasi dari [feature gap analysis](../feature-gap-analysis/):
- Jump dari Starter ke Pro = 3x harga (Rp 199k → Rp 599k), terlalu lebar
- Free terlalu sempit (cuma 100 kontak)
- Belum ada add-on / metered pricing untuk fitur baru (marketplace,
  mobile app, AI agent builder)

═══ RISET 2026 (BENCHMARK) ═══

- **3-4 tier psikologis** dengan tier tengah sebagai "most popular"
  → ARPU + conversion meningkat
- **A/B test pricing page kuartalan** → 12-18% lift per test
- **LTV:CAC ratio minimum 3:1** untuk sustainable
- **Payback period 80-90 hari** untuk SaaS healthy
- **Net Revenue Retention 95-120%** = benchmark VC-backed
- **CAC B2B SaaS 2026: $160-300 per lead** (Indonesia tier-2: ~Rp 750k-1.5jt)
- **5 model utama 2026:** value-based, freemium, usage-based, tier,
  outcome-based
- **AI features = real cost** → usage-based jadi populer untuk AI-heavy
  fitur

   - ARPU per tier
   - Churn rate per tier
   - Upgrade rate Free→Starter, Starter→Pro
   - Reverse: downgrade atau cancellation reason
   - Kalau gak ada data: apa proxy yang bisa dipakai?

2. COMPETITOR PRICING DEEP-DIVE
   Audit pricing page dan tier:
   - Mekari Qontak: tier breakdown, fitur per tier, harga
   - Qiscus: pricing model (per-agent? per-conversation?)
   - Wati: pricing tier, fitur
   - SleekFlow: pricing approach
   - YCloud, AiSensy, ChakraHQ: comparison
   
   Untuk masing-masing, ekstrak:
   - Entry price
   - Mid-tier price (yang setara dengan hallowa Pro)
   - Enterprise floor
   - Pricing model (flat? metered? hybrid?)
   - Limit per tier (kontak, user, message volume, channel)
   - Add-on / metered components
   - Free trial vs freemium
   - Annual vs monthly discount

3. POSITIONING & VALUE PROP MATRIX
   - hallowa positioned di mana di pricing landscape?
   - Premium vs value vs budget?
   - Apa "value moment" untuk masing-masing tier?
   - Apa yang justify upgrade?
   - Apakah pricing match dengan brand positioning?

4. RECOMMENDED PRICING STRUCTURE

   Option A — Tier Re-design (gradual):
   - Free: tetap, tapi 200 kontak (vs 100)
   - Starter: Rp 299k, 2k kontak, 3 user, +basic flow builder
   - Growth (NEW): Rp 599k, 5k kontak, 5 user, +CRM essentials,
     +1 marketplace, +mobile app
   - Pro: Rp 1.299k, 15k kontak, 10 user, +all marketplace,
     +flow builder advanced, +AI agent builder
   - Enterprise: Custom

   Option B — Hybrid Subscription + Metered:
   - Base subscription per tier
   - Plus per-conversation pricing (untuk volume tinggi)
   - Plus per-AI-conversation (karena AI ada real cost)
   - Plus add-on: per-marketplace integration

   Option C — Per-Agent Pricing (kompetitor common):
   - Rp 250-350k per agent per bulan
   - Volume discount untuk 10+ agent
   - Trade-off: lebih predictable revenue, tapi friction untuk scale

   Saranin yang paling fit untuk hallowa berdasarkan:
   - Customer profile (UMKM vs enterprise heavy?)
   - Sales motion (PLG vs sales-led?)
   - Cost structure (per-conversation cost?)
   - Competitor benchmark

5. ADD-ON & METERED PRICING

   Add-on candidates:
   - Marketplace integration: +Rp 99k/bulan per marketplace
     (Tokopedia, Shopee, Lazada, TikTok Shop)
   - Mobile app: include di Pro+ atau add-on Rp 49k/bulan untuk
     Starter/Growth?
   - AI Agent Builder: +Rp 199k/bulan
   - Extra WhatsApp number: +Rp 49k/nomor/bulan
   - Extra agent seat: +Rp 39k/seat/bulan
   - White-label: +Rp 999k/bulan (Enterprise add-on)
   - Premium support: +Rp 299k/bulan
   - Custom integration: per-project quote
   
   Metered:
   - Conversation overage: Rp 100/conversation di atas quota
   - WhatsApp template message: pass-through pricing dari Meta
     (Marketing/Utility/Authentication/Service)
   - AI conversation: Rp 50-200 per AI-handled conversation
     (depending on token usage)

6. PSYCHOLOGICAL PRICING TACTICS

   - **Decoy effect**: tier 3 (Pro) jadi terlihat better deal kalau
     Growth ditaro di tengah dengan value yang clear
   - **Anchoring**: tampilkan Enterprise duluan di pricing page
     (kasih "Custom" tanpa angka), bikin Pro keliatan affordable
   - **Charm pricing**: Rp 299k vs Rp 300k (test)
   - **Annual discount**: 2 bulan gratis kalau bayar tahunan = 17% off
   - **First-year promo**: 50% off bulan 1, normalize bulan 2+
   - **"Most Popular" badge** di Growth tier
   - **Comparison table** dengan checkmark visual yang konsisten
   - **Money-back guarantee 30 hari** (kepercayaan)

7. PRICING PAGE OPTIMIZATION

   Elements yang harus ada:
   - Comparison table dengan visual hierarchy
   - FAQ section pricing-specific
   - Calculator (estimasi cost berdasarkan volume)
   - Live chat untuk Enterprise inquiry
   - Social proof (logo customer, jumlah customer, testimoni)
   - Schema markup (Product, Offer, AggregateRating) untuk SEO

8. A/B TEST ROADMAP (kuartalan)

   Q1: Test tier structure baru vs lama
   Q2: Test charm pricing (Rp 299k vs Rp 300k)
   Q3: Test "Most Popular" badge placement
   Q4: Test annual discount messaging

9. MIGRATION PLAN (kalau ubah pricing)

   Critical: jangan kaget existing customer.
   - Grandfather existing customer di harga lama untuk 12 bulan
   - Beri opsi upgrade voluntary dengan diskon transition
   - Komunikasi 30 hari sebelum effective date
   - Highlight benefit baru yang mereka dapat
   - Honor cancellation request tanpa friction (preserves trust)

10. UNIT ECONOMICS VALIDATION

    Per tier, hitung:
    - Variable cost (Meta API cost, cloud cost, AI cost per conversation)
    - Gross margin target: minimum 70% untuk SaaS healthy
    - Contribution margin per customer
    - LTV (assume average tenure: Starter 18 bulan, Pro 24 bulan,
      Enterprise 36 bulan)
    - CAC realistic per tier (Free→paid conversion rate × CAC blended)
    - Payback period
