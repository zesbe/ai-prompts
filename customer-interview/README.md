# Customer Interview Script & Methodology — hallowa.id

Prompt untuk susun **customer interview script** untuk validate feature gap di hallowa.id sebelum invest development. Goal: 30+ interview dalam 4-5 minggu.

## 🎯 Why This Matters

Statistik: **50% feature yang di-build SaaS gak dipakai customer** (sumber: Pendo). Sebelum build dari [feature gap analysis](../feature-gap-analysis/), validate dulu via real customer voice.

## 📚 Framework yang Dipakai

### 1. The Mom Test (Rob Fitzpatrick)
3 aturan emas:
- **Talk about their LIFE**, not your idea
- Ask about **SPECIFIC PAST behavior**, not opinions/future
- **Talk LESS**, listen MORE

### 2. Jobs To Be Done (JTBD)
Customers don't buy products, they "hire" them for a job. Identify "job" yang hallowa hire-d untuk lakuin vs yang TIDAK ter-cover.

### 3. Commitment-Based Validation
Compliments worthless. Get COMMITMENT signals:
- **Time** (mau interview lagi?)
- **Reputation** (introduce ke peer?)
- **Cash** (mau beta-pay?)

## 📋 Scope

- 3 versi interview script:
  - **Existing customer** (paling penting — find churn risk + feature gap)
  - **Lost lead** (kompetitor advantage)
  - **Prospect** (Job-To-Be-Done, willingness to pay)
- Recruiting strategy + email templates
- Logistics (Calendly, tldv, Otter, Dovetail)
- Analysis methodology (tagging, frequency, severity scoring)
- Sample question bank per feature gap
- Output format (per-interview + aggregated report)
- Red flags & anti-bias checklist

## ❌ Mom Test Violations (red flags)

- ❌ "Apa pendapat lo tentang fitur X?" (opinion)
- ❌ "Kalau hallowa punya fitur Y, lo bakal pakai?" (hypothetical)
- ❌ "Setuju gak kalau fitur Z penting?" (leading)

## ✅ Mom Test Wins

- ✅ "Ceritain ke gue tentang terakhir kali lo handle [situation]. Step by step."
- ✅ "Last 30 hari, ada moment di mana lo frustrated dengan hallowa?"
- ✅ "Workaround apa yang lo punya sekarang?"
- ✅ "Kalau hallowa shutdown besok, apa yang lo lakuin?" (urgency)
